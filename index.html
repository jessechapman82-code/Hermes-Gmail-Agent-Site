Hermes Gmail Agent

Hermes Gmail Agent is a personal AI assistant integration that connects to Google services using OAuth 2.0.

Purpose

The application helps its owner retrieve, summarize, organize, and draft responses to Gmail messages. It may also access other Google Workspace services when authorized by the user.

How It Works

Google account access requires the user's explicit authorization. The integration runs on the user's own computer and stores OAuth credentials locally. Requested information may be processed by a configured local or cloud-based AI model.
