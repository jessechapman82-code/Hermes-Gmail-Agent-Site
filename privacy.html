Privacy Policy

Hermes Gmail Agent

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Overview

Hermes Gmail Agent is a personal-use integration operated independently of the Hermes developers. It connects an AI assistant to Google services with the account owner's authorization.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may access Gmail messages, metadata, labels, drafts, and other authorized Google Workspace data, including Drive files, calendars, contacts, and documents.

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized data is used to fulfill user requests, including searching and summarizing messages, organizing email, preparing drafts, and assisting with Google Workspace tasks.

4. AI Processing and Data Sharing

The integration operates on the owner's computer. Google data may be transmitted to the configured AI model provider when necessary to process a request. Processing may occur locally or through a cloud service, depending on the selected model. No Google account data is sold or used for advertising by the operator of this integration.

5. Data Storage and Security

Google OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Retrieved information may appear in local assistant conversation histories or logs. Security depends on the computer's configuration, access controls, and any connected AI services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored credentials and assistant conversation data can be deleted by the owner. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Cloud AI providers may have separate retention policies that apply when their services are used.

7. Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, where applicable.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
